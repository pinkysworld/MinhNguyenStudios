From the workshop

Ten worlds we’re building

Released or deep in production – this is what we’re pouring our hearts into right now.

On the App Store, Microsoft Store & Google Play

Summgarten

Singing garden · iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac & Windows · ages 3+

Your child’s voice becomes a seed: every recorded sound grows a plant or a little creature that sings it back in rhythm – until a tiny orchestra appears. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection.

On the Microsoft Store

Little Fixer (Kleiner Reparierer)

Workshop game · Windows · ages 3–6

Bruno the beaver runs a workshop, and the village animals bring him their broken things. Your child figures out what fits by trying: fixing, cleaning, painting – no reading required and no way to fail.

Visit the website →
In the works

Good Night, Toys

Interactive picture book · iPhone & iPad · ages 3+

Every toy had a big day. Your child gives each one a small, loving goodnight – a calm picture book to play along with, made for the last minutes before sleep.

Visit the website →
In preparation

Kontor

Economic & strategy game · iPad, Mac, PC

Hamburg, 1600. Your seal. Your risk. You run a trading house and invent insurance – dealing in danger and building your own statistics from letters, ship lists and death registers.

Visit the website →
In production

Germ Theory

Hospital building sim · Steam

“The germs are real. Nobody believes you.” Run a hospital in a port city between 1875 and 1892 – the years when medicine moved from miasma to microbes. Every discovery is real, every step forward hard-won.

In development

Breach Inc.

Management-Sim · Mac, PC, iPhone & iPad

The night shift is yours. Run a small IT security firm from a basement office: win clients, assign admins, buy protection – and decide how much margin to sacrifice before the next phishing wave hits.

In development

Sovereign Rig

City-building & political satire · PC (Steam & Microsoft Store)

Found your own state on a decommissioned oil platform in the North Sea. Build upwards deck by deck, keep crypto pioneers, an eco commune and tax exiles happy, and fight for what a micronation lacks most: recognition. With storm season, the coast guard and the state broadcaster “Radio Platform 1”.

In development

Lancet & Linen

Medieval life & practice simulation · PC (Steam & Microsoft Store)

Franconia, 1382: as a barber-surgeon you run a bathhouse and practice, treat patients with the means of the time, learn from masters, travel overland and keep your household together. The former browser game is becoming an extensive full version – thoroughly researched and open about where it simplifies.

In development

Brinehold (working title)

Island builder · PC

The water rises on a public tide table. Build dikes and sluices, move buildings and ferry residents to safety in time – before the highest tide arrives on the last day.

Prototype

Maladoro (working title)

Satirical clinic builder · PC

Build the most expensive private clinic in the Alps, invent ever costlier ailments for the rich and stall inspectors and lawyers. A wicked comedy about greed.
